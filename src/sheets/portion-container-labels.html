Why labels carry the load: at 4 p.m., on a hard day, "some casseroles in the fridge" is a puzzle. "CHICKEN SOUP — contains dairy — freeze by Friday — stovetop, medium heat, 6 min" is dinner. Write the reheating line even when it feels obvious.
Borrowed containers? Add your initials if you want them back, and tell the family they can hand pots to any volunteer. A labeled lid travels home with its owner eventually.
