Thank-You Note Starters

For when you're too tired for prose — fill in two blanks and it's a real note

Nobody expects a wedding-quality card for a pot of soup. One specific sentence beats ten generic ones. Pick a starter, fill the blanks, sign it. Done counts.

Openers — choose one
  • " The ________________ you brought on ______________ was exactly what we needed."
  • " You should know that ________________ was eaten by ______________ with ________________ of pure joy."
  • " I've thought about that ________________ every time the day got hard. Thank you for the ________________."
  • " You took a Tuesday off your own plate to feed ours. The ________________ didn't go unnoticed — it went fast."
Be specific — one detail makes it warm
  • " The kids asked for ________________ seconds, which never happens."
  • " We saved the ________________ and ate it the night of ______________, which was a rough one, and it helped."
  • " The ________________ you left in the cooler carried us through ________________."
  • " You even halved the spice for the kids and labeled everything — I can't tell you how much it meant not to think."
Close gently — no pressure, small promises
  • " We'd love to return the favor someday — when you're ready, dinner's ours: ________________."
  • " I'm too tired for fancy words, but please know we noticed everything. Love, ________________."
  • " Next time you have a hard week, dinner from us is already promised. I mean it: ________________."
