Food safety in one line: cold food cold (40 °F or below), hot food hot, don't leave perishables on a warm porch for hours, and when in doubt, toss it. A kind meal that makes someone sick is not a kind meal.
Put the thank-you sent column last on purpose: a two-line text written the same night beats a perfect note scheduled for a rainy day.
