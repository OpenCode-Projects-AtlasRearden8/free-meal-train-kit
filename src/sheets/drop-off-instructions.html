THE PORCH & COOLER ROUTINE

1. Text when you're on the way — a fifteen-minute heads-up is plenty.

2. Text again when you arrive. No reply means they may be feeding, sleeping, or resting. That's fine — the plan is the same either way.

3. Gently set the meal at the porch, or tuck it straight into the cooler if there's no answer.

4. One soft knock, then leave. Skip the doorbell — babies, surgery slots, and bad days all vote for quiet.

5. Send a photo text: what you left and where, so nobody has to guess or dig around.

6. Cooler logic: ice packs already inside; lid on tight; heavy container low; label facing up so they can read what's inside without moving it.

7. You do not have to come inside, take off your shoes, or stay five minutes. Dropping food off is the whole favor, and it's a real one.
