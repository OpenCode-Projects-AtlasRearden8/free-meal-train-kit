Before you sign up: check the dietary-needs grid first, then check for gaps rather than clusters — Mondays fill up, Tuesdays stay open. Store-bought counts. So does a pan of lasagna that covers two nights.
Signing up for the freezer? Mark "FOR FREEZER" in the "what I'm bringing" box. Freeze-ahead meals help most in weeks two through five, after the casseroles run out.
Can't cook? Sign up anyway: a gallon of milk, cut fruit, paper plates, cold drinks, or a $15 takeout gift card keep a household running exactly as well as a hot dish.
